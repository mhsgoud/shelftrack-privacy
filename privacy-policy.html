Privacy Policy — Shelf Track

Last updated: June 2026

Shelf Track (“the app”) helps you scan barcodes, look up product information, manage grocery lists, track pantry stock, and monitor best-before dates. We respect your privacy.

Who is responsible?

Mohsen Goudarzi
Email: goudarzi.mohsen@gmail.com

What data do we process?

  • Camera — used only to scan barcodes and read expiry dates on labels when you choose to. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Barcode (EAN) — sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product name, brand, and image where available.
  • On your phone — shopping lists, purchase history, pantry items, and product cache (SQLite). This data stays on your device unless you back up your phone yourself.
  • Advertising — the free version shows banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage data according to Google’s privacy policy. You can learn more at Google AdMob & privacy.

What we do not collect

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, home address, or precise location. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party services

  • Open Food Facts — open product database
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising
  • Google Play — app distribution

Retention

Data stored on your phone remains until you delete the app or clear data in the app. Third-party services maintain their own logs and retention policies.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because most data is stored locally, you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing lists and history inside the app. For questions, contact goudarzi.mohsen@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do.
