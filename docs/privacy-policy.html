Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

ShelfTrack (“the app”) helps you scan barcodes, compare grocery prices, manage shopping lists, track pantry stock, read expiry dates, and ask an AI assistant about products and stores. We respect your privacy and keep most data on your device.

Who is responsible?

ShelfTrack (the app publisher)
Contact: shelftrack.support@gmail.com

What data is stored on your phone?

The app stores data locally in SQLite on your device. This includes:

  • Shopping lists and list items
  • Purchase history and pantry / kitchen inventory
  • Cached product information and price snapshots
  • AI chat history in the Ask AI tab (until you clear the chat or uninstall the app)
  • App settings and an anonymous device identifier used for AI usage limits

This data stays on your phone unless you back up your device yourself. We do not sync it to our own cloud.

What data is sent to services when you use the app?

  • Camera — used when you scan barcodes or read expiry dates on labels. Images are processed on your device (OCR). We do not upload photos to our servers.
  • Barcode (EAN) — sent to Open Food Facts to look up product name, brand, and image where available.
  • AI assistant (Ask AI) — when you send a message, the app sends your chat messages, optional scanned product context, your device language and country/region (not GPS), an anonymous device ID, and subscription status to our backend API. Our API processes requests using OpenAI. The assistant may also use web search for store links and product information when needed. Chat usage is limited per month; we store quota counts linked to your anonymous device ID on our server.
  • Voice add — when you use speech to add list items, audio is sent to our API for transcription (same authentication as Ask AI). We do not keep your recordings after transcription.
  • Shared lists (optional) — if you enable live sharing, list contents and join codes sync through our backend so household members can edit together. Sharing is opt-in per list.
  • Subscriptions — if you subscribe to Pro, purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. We receive subscription status (for example, whether Pro is active), not your payment card details.
  • Analytics & crash reporting (optional) — when enabled, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand feature usage (for example: screens opened, list created, shopping started, paywall shown) and to diagnose crashes. Events are anonymous and do not include your grocery item names, list contents, or photos. You can learn more in Google’s privacy policy.
  • Advertising — banner ads via Google AdMob are currently disabled. If ads are re-enabled later, AdMob may collect device and usage data according to Google’s privacy policy.
  • Product catalog (optional) — if enabled, the app may query a read-only product catalog hosted on Supabase to show store prices and promotions. Queries typically include product identifiers or search terms, not personal profile data.
  • Feedback email — if you choose “Send feedback” in Settings, your email app opens with our support address. We only receive what you choose to send.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account to use the app.
  • We do not collect your name, home address, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Third-party services

  • Open Food Facts — product database
  • OpenAI — AI assistant replies (via our API)
  • Google Firebase — analytics and crash reporting (when enabled)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising (currently disabled)
  • Supabase — optional product catalog and shared-list hosting
  • Google Play — app distribution and in-app purchases

Retention

  • On your phone — until you delete data in the app, clear chat, or uninstall.
  • AI quota on our server — usage counters are kept per month for fair-use limits.
  • Shared lists — while a list is shared; leaving or stopping sharing removes your access.
  • Third parties — each provider keeps data according to its own policy.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and to object or lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Because most data is stored locally, you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing lists, history, and chat inside the app. For questions about data we process on our servers, contact shelftrack.support@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
