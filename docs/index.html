Everything you need for grocery shopping — in one Android app

ShelfTrack combines a smart shopping list, barcode scanner, pantry tracker, and expiry reminders so you buy what you need, use what you have, and throw away less.

  • Smart shopping lists

    Build weekly grocery lists, check items off in the store, add products by typing or voice, and keep multiple lists for different shops or meal plans.

  • Barcode scanner

    Scan product barcodes to look up names and details quickly — no more guessing aisles or retyping long labels from packaging.

  • Live shared lists

    Share a list with a six-digit join code so partners or housemates see the same groceries in real time. Perfect for shared shopping and last-minute adds.

  • Pantry & expiry tracking

    Save what you bought, scan best-before dates from labels, and get reminders for items going off soon — so food gets eaten before it spoils.

  • Receipt scanning (Pro)

    Photograph your receipt after checkout to match prices to your list and build a simple purchase history of what you actually paid.

  • Ask AI about products

    Not sure what to buy or how to use an ingredient? Ask the built-in assistant — optionally with barcode context from something you scanned.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ShelfTrack grocery app. More detail on the support page.

Is ShelfTrack a free grocery list app?

Yes. You can download ShelfTrack free on Google Play. The free version includes barcode scanning, Ask AI (with fair usage limits), and up to two lists with 40 items each. Pro adds receipt matching, kitchen and expiry tracking, unlimited lists, and more.

Can I share a shopping list with my partner or family?

Yes. Enable live sharing on a list to get a short join code. Others install ShelfTrack, tap Join list, and enter the code. Changes sync while the shared list is open on both phones.

Does ShelfTrack track expiry dates and best-before labels?

With Pro, you can save best-before dates by scanning labels or entering dates manually. ShelfTrack shows items going off soon in a separate section from expired products, so you know what to use first.

Where is my grocery data stored?

Lists, pantry items, and purchase history are stored locally on your Android phone. Optional online features (shared lists, AI, analytics) only send what is needed for those features. Read our privacy policy for details.

Which phones does ShelfTrack support?

ShelfTrack is available for Android on Google Play. You need a camera for barcode and receipt scanning. Microphone access is optional and only used when you add items by voice.
