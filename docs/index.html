How it works

One app for the trip to the store and what happens after you get home.

  • Scan

    Look up barcodes, capture prices and expiry dates from labels, and add products without retyping names.

  • Lists

    Plan weekly shops, share live lists with household join codes, or add items by voice while your hands are full.

  • Stock & expiry

    See what you bought, what you still have at home, and what’s going off soon — so less food ends up wasted.

  • Ask AI

    Ask about products and stores with optional barcode context. Usage is limited fairly; most of your grocery data stays on device.

Built for real shopping

No account required for the core experience. Shopping lists, pantry, and purchase history live in SQLite on your phone. We don’t sell your personal data.
